What Is a Corporate Video?

As the name implies, a corporate video is simply a video produced by a business or an organization for its own use. There are many ways in which corporate videos can be used in a business setting and for a variety of purposes.

Corporate videos are generally used to promote and raise awareness for a brand as a whole. They can also provide a visual way for a business to communicate with customers and other audiences.

Businesses looking for corporate video production can explore this page and the Pace Media portfolio.
